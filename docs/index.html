About

Mattia

I'm a freelance graphic designer with over seven years of experience designing and adapting creative assets for print and digital media. Currently Visual Media Editor at European Schoolnet, alongside running my independent practice, Design.Kreatively, since 2018.

I hold a degree in Graphic Design from LUCA School of Arts, Brussels, and work across editorial layout, posters, and visual identity for education, culture, technology and rights-related sectors.
