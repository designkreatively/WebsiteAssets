About me

Design Kreatively → Mattia

Hi there! My name is Mattia and I'm a seasoned graphic designer based in Brussels. I specialise in creating impactful illustrations and knowledge-sharing materials that engage audiences. I work with NGOs, cultural organisations, public institutions, private companies and EU-funded projects, coordinating with communications officers, project managers, external contractors and stakeholders across multicultural and multilingual teams.

My mission is to blend creativity with a strategic understanding of client needs, ensuring each design is both effective and adaptable. My background combines training in Media and Information Design with a Master's in International Relations, which helps me turn complex multilingual policy content into clear, accessible visuals.
