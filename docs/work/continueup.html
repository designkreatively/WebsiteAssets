Infographic Illustration · Illustration Series · 2025

ContinueUP | Illustrations

For the ContinueUP project, I created a series of infographics designed to communicate key ideas about inclusive and digitally competent teaching. The illustrations bring together visual clarity and a consistent, human-centered style that reflects the educational purpose of the initiative. My goal was to translate complex concepts into accessible visuals while conveying a careful narrative. Furthermore, the illustrations were translated into 9 languages.
