Still Human. Are you sure?

Recent submission to Posterheroes 15, "Still Human?"; shortlisted but not selected.

In "Still Human?" I depict the gradual erosion of human imagination, curiosity, and intellect in the age of AI. The scene shows a common exchange with an LLM, but here the machine takes control, typing 'Still Human? Are you sure?' by itself. Human hands remain distant, shocked, semi-transparent, and fading away, symbolising the loss of human agency and intellectual presence.
