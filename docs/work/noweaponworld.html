Satirical Illustration · Poster · 2024

NoWeaponWorld Poster (Warmart)

This exhibition explored the marketisation of war. For the artwork 'Warmart,' the global arms trade is reimagined as a cheerful supermarket flyer, advertising weapons with a 'Kill 1 Get 1 Free' offer.

A rifle sits on a bright yellow background, using the visual language of retail advertising to parody how commercial design can normalise profit-driven destruction.
