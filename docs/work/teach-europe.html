Visual Identity · Branding · 2024

Teach Europe Logo and Visual Identity

The logo combines the EU flag and its member countries to represent European identity, further emphasized by the inclusion of a fingerprint symbolizing individuality within unity. Circles are used to represent teachers, fostering a sense of collaboration and community, while a pen serves as a powerful symbol of teaching, knowledge, and education. More visuals present on the Teach Europe website.
