BIK MOOC | Cyberbullying in a Rapidly Changing Digital World

Key visual designed for the Better Internet for Kids (BIK) MOOC on cyberbullying. An educator and a student navigating a split digital landscape: on one side, a looming figure holding a phone filled with hostile content; on the other, a warm, welcoming space where positive interactions flourish. The adult figure guides an informed choice, steering toward healthier online behaviours rather than simply shielding from harm.
