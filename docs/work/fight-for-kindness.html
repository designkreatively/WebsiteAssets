Fight for Kindness

This work has been selected for the Fight for Kindness exhibition at the House of Lucie Gallery in Budapest, 13–27 November 2026.

Fight for Kindness is TypeCampus's international poster call, in which designers interpret kindness through typography. Selected posters are shown in exhibitions around the world each year on World Kindness Day, 13 November.

For this edition I made a series of three typographic posters inspired by "Live and let live": Let Be, Let Feel, Let Speak.

Series description: A 3-piece typographic series exploring empathy as a shared practice, through feeling, listening, and freedom. Each artwork isolates one human gesture, then turns it into a simple visual rule for kinder coexistence.

Learn more about Fight for Kindness at TypeCampus →

Artwork 1 — Feel and Let Feel. A reflection on emotional permission, and the importance of allowing feelings to exist without judgment.

Artwork 2 — Listen and Let Speak. A quiet reminder that understanding begins with listening, and making space for other voices.

Artwork 3 — Be and Let Be. A statement about freedom, respect, and the dignity of letting others exist as they are.
