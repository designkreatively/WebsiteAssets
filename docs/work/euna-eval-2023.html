EUN Academy Evaluation Report 2023

The 2023 EUN Academy Evaluation report had courses focusing on environment, on AI, games, technology. The illustration moved towards the themes of Environment, AI, and Technology: a teacher with a transparent smart device, a lush natural landscape with biophilic buildings as the background, and futuristic tech feeling to simulate AI.
