Eminent 25 - AI in Schools

For last year's event “Eminent 2025”, the theme put AI at the center of the conference. Now, the challenge was to show AI in education without going too literal, but also avoiding the generic, abstract tech stuff. A sphere with the AI icon glowing, inspired by the way voice-based AI (like ChatGPT audio) visually responds to input. The icon is incorporated in a sort of brain-eye display, inside a futuristic floating 3D sphere. How did I do it? 1. Found a photo of teachers in a classroom. 2. Created the Sparkle Star icon. 3. Used Adobe Dimension to shape it in 3D. 4. Added some magic touch with Adobe Firefly.
